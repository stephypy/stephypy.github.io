stephypy is typing...

the closest thing to a blog i'll ever make

I write for the sake of writing and every once in awhile I end up with a cohesive piece of free verse that I don't mind sharing to the world. Below I have included five pieces (both english and spanish) that were the product of this past winter. Please enjoy.

april 2026: Lately, I've been listening to The Strokes non-stop so I find it necessary to display one of their songs here. Thank you Julian Casablancas for reigniting my insufferable personality.

Escarlata

el piso quedo manchado con la violencia de mi utero
otra batalla fallida por mi falta de lujuria
pero me rehuso usar mi vientre sin la promesa
que mi hija sea de otra tierra
la luna continuara obligarma a vivir
entre rios escarlata
hasta que me enamore de la persona indicada

Omen for Grasshoppers

beware of the sound of the cymbal
a bright crash for a melodious night
remniscent of a hollow echo of a tymbal
a song to sway the masses from their fight and flight


call on the cicadas to cease their spell
their warm and bright tones lullabying grasshoppers into locusts
swarming begets an invasion difficult to quell
beware of violence unnoticed


clicking, cackling, and corroding
the cicadas forge a new era
lethal, leeching, and lurking
the locusts consume and plague their path


cacophonies lead to substantial torment
but noises can be drowned with some hopping
the fate of grasshoppers relied on whether they submit
after all, grass is only greener when you water it

Particula

que peculiar fue nuestro encuentro
coincidiendo con la madrugada
una particula de familiaridad
que desperto mi romance por dentro
y por semanas espere tu llamada


fueron tus encantos que sacudieron mi epicentro
descuide mis veladas -- yo completamente desarmada
sone una vida contigo a pesar de tu brevedad
consumi tus migajas sin la promesa de un reencuentro
entre duda y anhelo me dejaste en una encrucijada


tu diminuta atencion es en lo que me concentro
tus gramos de devocion me mantiene embriagada
que particular ha sido nuestra amistad
una donde tu dominas y yo soy engendro
yo que te di totalidad para ser amada


tu -- una particula que ni siquiera fue semilla

Mile High Club

has the pilot issued a turbulence warning
or is this turmoil caused by your presence?
providence bestowed this seat with your essence
our knees touching was the start of my sentence


you were the embodiment of midnight
your lips parted, I was engulfed by your insistence
providence commanded us to reach her heavens
you left your seat I quickly followed your allegiance


we were two bodies melded in a narrow space
my face between your legs exploring your brilliance
your chest heaved and your hips moved with elegance
my tongue was glistening with your conscience


providence came one last time for the climax
your fingers fought to make me lose my coherence
our moanings reached an equal cadence
we sealed our urgency with a kiss; a final evidence

me tuve que imaginar

me tuve que imaginar un final porque nunca me contestaste
y quede varada y en delirio
y ya que no me otorgas una muerte apiadada
mejor busco un alivio en lo ficticio
dire que me amaste y me acurrucaste
dire que tu encendiste todos mis latidos
dire que me besaste y me deseaste
y que jamas te fuiste a otra parte
