the closest thing to a blog i'll ever make

I write for the sake of writing and every once in awhile I end up a cohesive piece of free verse that I don't mind sharing to the world. Below I have included four pieces (both english and spanish) that were the product of this past winter. Please enjoy.

omen for grasshoppers

beware of the sound of the cymbal a bright crash for a melodious night remniscent of a hollow echo of a tymbal a song to sway the masses from their fight and flight call on the cicadas to cease their spell their warm and bright tones lullabying grasshoppers into locusts swarming begets an invasion difficult to quell beware of violence unnoticed clicking, cackling, and corroding the cicadas forge a new era lethal, leeching, and lurking the locusts consume and plague their path cacophonies lead to substantial torment but noises can be drowned with some hopping the fate of grasshoppers relied on whether they submit after all, grass is only greener when you water it

particula

que peculiar fue nuestro encuentro coincidiendo con la madrugada una particula de familiaridad que desperto mi romance por dentro y por semanas espere tu llamada fueron tus encantos que sacudieron mi epicentro descuide mis veladas -- yo completamente desarmada sone una vida contigo a pesar de tu brevedad consumi tus migajas sin la promesa de un reencuentro entre duda y anhelo me dejaste en una encrucijada tu diminuta atencion es en lo que me concentro tus gramos de devocion me mantiene embriagada que particular ha sido nuestra amistad una donde tu dominas y yo soy engendro yo que te di totalidad para ser amada tu -- una particula que ni siquiera fue semilla

mile high club

has the pilot issued a turbulence warning or is this turmoil caused by your presence? providence bestowed this seat with your essence our knees touching was the start of my sentence you were the embodiment of midnight your lips parted, I was engulfed by your insistence providence commanded us to reach her heavens you left your seat I quickly followed your allegiance we were two bodies melded in a narrow space my face between your legs exploring your brilliance your chest heaved and your hips moved with elegance my tongue was glistening with your conscience providence came one last time for the climax your fingers fought to make me lose my coherence our moanings reached an equal cadence we sealed our urgency with a kiss; a final evidence

me tuve que imaginar un final

me tuve que imaginar un final porque nunca me contestaste y quede varada y en delirio y ya que no me otorgas una muerte apiadada mejor busco un alivio en lo ficticio dire que me amaste y me acurrucaste dire que tu encendiste todos mis latidos dire que me besaste y me deseaste y que jamas te fuiste a otra parte
